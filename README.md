# BILLQORA - Supermarket POS & Billing Software

**BILLQORA** is a supermarket POS and billing software designed for
supermarkets and retail stores.

🌐 **Official Website:** https://billqora-pos.netlify.app/

💻 **Windows Application:** Download BILLQORA from the official website.

BILLQORA provides:
- Fast POS billing
- Inventory management
- Customer management
- Loyalty points
- Invoice management
- Cash register
- Sales and business reports
- Automatic updates
